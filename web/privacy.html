SafeTemp privacy

SafeTemp tells you how dangerous today's weather is for you. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it never sells data.

Kept only on your phone or computer

  • The people you save: names, ages, health conditions and identities.
  • Your saved city, map settings and checklist answers.

Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data) deletes these.

Sent to SafeTemp's server, but not stored

  • A health condition or description you type, so it can be turned into sensitivity numbers.
  • For the optional AI advice: today's weather numbers and a short description of who it's for.

If AI advice is turned on, these are passed to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to write the advice. Names are never sent.

Stored on SafeTemp's server, only if you turn on alerts

  • Your device's notification address, the location you're checking and its town name, so we can send official weather alerts for that place.

Turning alerts off, or turning off notifications for SafeTemp, deletes these.

Services SafeTemp uses

To check the weather, your location (or a city you pick) is sent directly to these services:

  • Open-Meteo: weather and air quality forecasts.
  • BigDataCloud: town names for a location, and an approximate location from your internet connection when GPS is off.
  • US National Weather Service: official weather alerts.
  • OpenFreeMap: map tiles. Google Fonts and cdnjs: fonts and the map code.
  • Expo, Apple and Google: delivering notifications, if you turn on alerts.

Not medical advice

SafeTemp gives general safety information based on National Weather Service, CDC, EPA and WHO guidance. It doesn't replace official warnings, a doctor or 911.
