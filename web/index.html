              Get help

              • Call 911 if someone is confused, faints, stops sweating, has numb white skin, or can't breathe well.
              • Call 211 (free, 24/7, US) for cooling and warming centers, shelters and coat drives.
              • Help paying heating and cooling bills (LIHEAP).

              About the numbers

              Heat and cold use the National Weather Service heat index and wind chill charts, adjusted for groups the CDC lists as higher risk. UV follows the WHO scale, air quality the EPA AQI, wind the NWS advisory levels. Weather from Open-Meteo; US alerts from the National Weather Service.

              SafeTemp gives general safety information. It doesn't replace official warnings or 911. Privacy
